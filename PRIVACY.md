# Vannamayam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Vannamayam is a photo editor that works entirely on your device.

- **No data collection.** Vannamayam does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
- **No internet access.** The app does not request internet permission, so your photos never leave your phone.
- **Photos.** Vannamayam only opens the photos you choose with the Android photo picker, and saves your edited copies to `Pictures/Vannamayam` on your device. Originals are never modified.
- **No ads, no analytics, no accounts.**

If this ever changes (for example, if optional cloud features are added), this policy will be updated before the change ships.
